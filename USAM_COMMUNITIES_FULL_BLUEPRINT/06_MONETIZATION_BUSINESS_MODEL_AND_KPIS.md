**Purpose:** Launch pricing and measurable validation  
**Applies to:** USAM Communities React/Node.js/EC2 package  
**Sources:** audited source at commit lineage `03ea43c...`, Universal Blueprint v1.1, supplied master prompt, official sources ledger  
**Status:** CURRENT + TARGET

## Price hypotheses

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Intended buyer |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | EGP 0 | EGP 0 | exploration and baseline participation |
| Plus | EGP 149 | EGP 1,490 | active individual member |
| Pro | EGP 349 | EGP 3,490 | founder/freelancer/professional needing closer support |
| Organization | EGP 1,499 | EGP 14,990 | company, university or community team |

Annual pricing includes two months relative to monthly. No “limited-time” claim is shown. Measure visitor→signup, signup→first value, 30/90-day active retention, plan-view→request, request→verified payment, cancellation reason, program utilization and support load. North Star: monthly members completing one meaningful professional action with another person.
