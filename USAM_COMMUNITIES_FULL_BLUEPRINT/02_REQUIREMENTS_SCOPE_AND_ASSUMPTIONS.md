# Requirements, Scope and Assumptions

**Purpose:** Source-of-truth requirements and change boundaries  
**Applies to:** USAM Communities React/Node.js/EC2 package  
**Sources:** audited source at commit lineage `03ea43c...`, Universal Blueprint v1.1, supplied master prompt, official sources ledger  
**Status:** CURRENT + TARGET

## Non-negotiable requirements

1. Preserve the working design and existing functionality.
2. Arabic and English with RTL/LTR, light/dark and responsive layouts.
3. Public discovery plus a member workspace for four official communities.
4. Real persistence and server authorization in EC2 mode; honest device-local behavior on Sites.
5. Monthly and annual subscriptions with multiple options.
6. Admin review, safety, legal summaries, accessibility and EC2 runbooks.
7. No invented users, partners, payments, testimonials or production claims.

## Assumptions

- Currency is EGP and primary market is Egypt.
- Annual plans charge the equivalent of ten months.
- Prices are launch experiments reviewed after real activation/retention data.
- Paymob merchant enablement is a release dependency, not assumed.
