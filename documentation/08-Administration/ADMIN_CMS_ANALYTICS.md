# Administration, moderation and analytics

## Implemented admin operations

- Real overview counts for users, published posts/events, open reports, pending organizations and open contact requests.
- Create free events, offers and challenges.
- Review reports, organization submissions, mentor profiles, challenge submissions and contact requests.
- Audit entries for the implemented review and publishing mutations.

## Missing administration domains

User suspension/appeal, community manager roles, organization teams, partner lifecycle, room application review/grouping/check-in, ticket refunds, CMS pages, feature flags, prompt/template management, email templates, retention tools, exports/deletion, audit viewer, analytics filters and tenant-level dashboards.

## Analytics policy

Only database-derived counts may be displayed. No fake growth, attendance, conversion, partner or revenue figures. A production analytics engine requires a documented event taxonomy, consent basis, retention window, access controls and a selected storage/observability provider.
